Time does not stand still and nowhere is that more obvious than in the Galerie Vivienne in Paris. Within a single century, this place experienced birth, glory, decline, and rebirth. It is a space defined by transformation. The mosaic floor, which runs through the entire gallery, became my medium. A surface walked on for generations, carrying the marks of time. The result is a publication that doesn't just document the Galerie Vivienne — it tries to embody it.

Some things are designed once and never meant to be touched again. Conceived nearly a century ago, they still feel like they belong to tomorrow. This catalogue sits at the intersection of past and future, exploring what makes a design truly timeless. The result is not just a document of three iconic lamps, it is a reflection on the space between then and now.

One hour, every day. The commute to Emmenbrücke is reliable… until it isn't. This brochure is a portrait of a place shaped by traffic, delays, and the quiet frustration of getting from A to B. But rather than documenting the problem, it imagines alternatives — bikes on rail tracks, buses in bicycle lanes. A small, slightly absurd exploration of what it means to depend on something that doesn't always show up.
